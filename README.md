# Fallen's Modifier Keys and Action Bar Pages

Turn any key or mouse button into a modifier, for World of Warcraft. Hold it, or tap it, and the keys you press abilities with press another action bar. Or have a bar switch to another bar's abilities in place.

1. Type `/fmk`. The setup opens by itself the first time.
2. Set your action keys: the keys of one of your bars, or your own, in twelve boxes. Click a box and press its key.
3. Click Add a modifier key and press the key or mouse button. Pick Hold or Tap, and the bar it goes to.

While the modifier is on, that bar lights up and shows your keys on its buttons.

With Tap, tap the key and then press one key: 1 then 1 is that bar's first button.

A modifier key can also switch a bar in place: the bar shows another bar's abilities, and your keys stay where they are. Use the plus on a key's line to switch more than one bar with the same key.

A key used as a modifier stops doing what it did before. Take it off the list and it is back.

The setup can be reached later with `/fmk`, the minimap button, or the addon's page in the game's options.
