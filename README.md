# Fallen's Modifier Keys

Turn any key or mouse button into a modifier, for World of Warcraft. Hold it and the keys you press abilities with press another action bar. Let go and they are your own again.

1. Type `/fmk`. The window opens by itself the first time.
2. Pick your action keys: the keys of one of your bars, or keys of your own.
3. Click Add a hold key and press the key or mouse button you want to hold. Pick the bar it goes to.

While you hold the key, that bar lights up and shows your keys on its buttons.

A hold key can also switch a bar in place: the bar shows another bar's abilities while the key is down, and your keys stay where they are. Use the plus on a hold key's line to switch more than one bar with the same key.

A key used as a hold key stops doing what it did before. Take it off the list and it is back.
